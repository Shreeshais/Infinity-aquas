04 / THE PROCESS

From blank bottle
to brand moment.

One clear path from the first conversation to finished bottles, whether the run is ten or ten thousand.

01

Brief

Share your vision, bottle silhouette, target quantity, event timeline, and branding cues. Whether you arrive with a complete identity guide or a rough moodboard, we map the technical wrap specifications, material finishes, and production roadmap — tailored to your occasion.

→
02

Design

Our studio transforms your brand into bespoke 360-degree wrap concepts. Typography, colour palettes, foil accents and tactile textures are meticulously balanced. You receive high-resolution visual mockups so you can see how your bottle commands a room before a single print is made.

→
03

Approve

You review artwork layouts in fine detail, specify any last adjustments, and sign off on the final production proof. We calibrate print profiles, verify colour accuracy against your brand standards, and ensure every curve and bleed aligns flawlessly before fabrication begins.

→
04

Production & Delivery

Using high-grade waterproof substrates and precision application techniques, each bottle is individually wrapped, inspected to our exacting quality standard, and carefully boxed. Your custom bottles arrive ready to serve, display and create unforgettable impressions — on time.
